## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Khobra Cleaning website, customer booking service, native mobile applications, mobile web wrapper, and the operational portals used by authorized customers, administrators, cleaners, drivers, and other personnel.

It explains what personal data we process, why we process it, who may receive it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights available to you.

## 3. Personal data we process

Depending on how you use Khobra Cleaning, we may process:

- **Account and profile data:** name, email address, telephone number, password hash, profile photo, role, account status, and last login information.
- **Address, optional GPS, and booking data:** saved addresses, service address, city, area, optional precise latitude and longitude when you choose phone GPS, service requested, appointment date and time, duration, crew size, booking notes, preferences, recurrence, status, cancellation or no-show information, and staff assignments.
- **Payment and transaction data:** payment method, invoice and payment status, amounts, bank transfer reference, bank name, account holder name, transfer date, payment proof, verification remarks, and transaction history. The current service does not collect payment card numbers.
- **Communications and content:** complaints, descriptions, responses, resolution notes, ratings, comments, and files or images you choose to upload.
- **Notification data:** in-app notification records, browser push subscriptions, and mobile push tokens used to deliver notifications.
- **Workforce and operational data:** for cleaners, drivers, administrators, and other personnel, contact details, addresses, employee or driver identifiers, skills, attendance, leave, payroll or salary information, driver licence and vehicle information, trips, mileage, expenses, fuel costs, and receipts.
- **Technical and security data:** IP address used for login, signup, public booking rate limits and security, session identifiers, request identifiers, device or browser information processed by security services, and limited server error records.
- **Device permissions and local storage:** foreground location permission only when you choose phone GPS, notification permission when you choose to enable notifications, secure device storage for the native app session, and limited local storage used by the mobile web wrapper for push notification status, tokens, and pending navigation.

Manual address entry remains available. The app requests a one-time foreground GPS reading only after you select **Use phone GPS**. It stores the resulting latitude and longitude with the saved service address and booking. It does not collect background or continuous location. We do not currently use advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising trackers, contacts, microphone, or broad device storage access.

- process and verify payments and maintain invoices and transaction records;
- provide customer support and resolve complaints or disputes;
- manage authorized workforce operations, attendance, payroll, dispatch, expenses, and access permissions;
- prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and excessive automated requests;
- maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability and security of the service; and
- comply with legal, accounting, regulatory, and law enforcement obligations.

We do not use personal data for third-party behavioral advertising and we do not sell personal data.

## 5. Legal grounds

Where the UAE Federal Personal Data Protection Law applies, we process personal data on one or more of the following grounds:

- processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you, including creating an account, handling a booking, receiving payment, and providing the requested service;
- processing is necessary to comply with an applicable legal obligation;
- processing is necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, protect the service, or prevent fraud and misuse where permitted by law;

When consent is the applicable ground, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing permitted on another legal ground.
